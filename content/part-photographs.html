From program renderings to photographs

Each pair below shows a rendering drawn by a world’s program and a photograph that an image-generation model produced from that rendering. A world passes only if the same pixel-only inverse program recovers the expected answer from all five renderings and all five photographs. In a two-hour run, 20 of 40 worlds passed; 11 of them are shown here, with 54 image pairs.

This is a first feasibility experiment run after submission, not a benchmark. These are development samples: the check is automatic, there was no formal human review, and a pass does not certify that a photograph is free of other artifacts. The worlds shown are a selection of the passing ones, and the pairs were screened by eye before inclusion, so a world may show fewer than five pairs.
